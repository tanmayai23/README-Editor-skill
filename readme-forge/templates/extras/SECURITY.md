## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report privately through [GitHub Security Advisories]({{REPO_URL}}/security/advisories/new) or email **{{SECURITY_EMAIL}}**. Include:

- the affected version and component
- steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- the impact as you understand it

We will acknowledge your report within {{N}} business days and keep you informed until it's resolved. With your permission, we will credit you in the release notes.
