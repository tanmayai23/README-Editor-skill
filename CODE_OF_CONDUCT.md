# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include empathy and kindness, respecting differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for our mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@tanmayai23](https://github.com/tanmayai23), through a [private advisory](https://github.com/tanmayai23/README-Editor-skill/security/advisories/new), which only maintainers can see. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.
